About Us

Your Trusted Partner in Tire Repair Solutions

Trusted Enterprise – Buy with Confidence

Hebei Century Auto Parts Co., Ltd. was established in 2010. We are a professional manufacturer and supplier specializing in comprehensive tire repair products, with particular expertise in tire valves, wheel weights, tire repair seals, tire repair patches, and tire repair tools.

Our commitment is to providing one-stop solutions for business partners across the globe. With over 12 years of dedicated service, we have successfully delivered our products to more than 50 countries, earning widespread recognition and trust from customers worldwide.
