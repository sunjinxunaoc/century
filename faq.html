Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about wheel weights, tire seals, tire patches and more

What types of wheel weights do you manufacture?

We manufacture clip-on and adhesive wheel weights in three materials: Fe (steel) — galvanized for rust resistance, Zn (zinc) — premium corrosion resistant, and Pb (lead) — traditional vibration damping. Our wheel weights range from 5g to 200g and are suitable for passenger cars, trucks, buses and motorcycles. Browse our wheel weights →

What is the difference between clip-on and adhesive wheel weights?

Clip-on wheel weights attach to the rim flange of steel wheels using a metal clip. Adhesive (stick-on) wheel weights use 3M adhesive backing to attach to the inner surface of alloy/aluminum wheels. Clip-on weights are used for steel rims; adhesive weights are designed for alloy rims. View clip-on weights → View adhesive weights →

What tire seal and tire patch products do you offer?

We offer a complete range: tire seal strips for emergency puncture repairs, mushroom patch plugs for permanent internal repairs, Euro-style radial patches for radial tires, bias patches for bias-ply tires, and tubeless tire patches. Browse all tire seals & patches →

Our MOQ varies by product category. For standard wheel weights and tire patches, we offer flexible MOQ options suitable for both small distributors and large wholesalers. Contact us with your requirements for the best solution.

What is a tire seal strip used for?

A tire seal strip is used for quick emergency puncture repairs on tubeless tires. The rubber strip is inserted into the puncture using a repair tool, creating an immediate seal — ideal for roadside emergencies. View tire seal strips →

What is the difference between a tire patch and a tire plug?

A tire plug (seal strip) is inserted from outside for quick temporary repairs. A tire patch is applied to the inner liner for permanent repairs. Mushroom patch plugs combine both — a stem fills the puncture and a patch bonds inside for the most reliable permanent repair. View mushroom plug patches →
