TPMS Valve Sensors & Metal Clamp-In Valves

Century Auto Parts manufactures the complete TPMS (Tire Pressure Monitoring System) range: TPMS-1 to TPMS-50 series valve sensors and MS525AL / MS636AL metal clamp-in TPMS valves with round or flat bases.

All TPMS valves feature rim hole Φ11.5/0.453", grommet RG54, washer RW15, nut HN9 and cap ET, compatible with major vehicle brands.
