Clip-On Wheel Weight

Precision-Balanced Clip-On Weights in Fe, Zn & Pb — Factory Direct

High-Quality Clip-On Wheel Weights for Steel Rims

Century Auto Parts manufactures precision clip-on wheel weights in three materials: Fe (steel), Zn (zinc) and Pb (lead). Our clip-on weights are designed for steel rims on passenger cars, trucks, buses and commercial vehicles.

With over 12 years of manufacturing experience, we produce clip-on wheel weights that meet international quality standards. Each weight is tested for accurate weight tolerance, proper clip tension, and consistent finish quality.

Available in weights from 5g to 200g (0.25oz to 7oz), covering all standard balance weight series for global markets.
