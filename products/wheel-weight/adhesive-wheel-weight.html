Adhesive Wheel Weight

Stick-On Wheel Weights for Alloy Rims — Fe, Zn & Pb with 3M Backing

Premium Adhesive Wheel Weights for Alloy Rims

Century Auto Parts is a professional adhesive wheel weight manufacturer producing high-quality stick-on weights for alloy rims. Our adhesive wheel weights feature strong 3M adhesive backing for reliable, long-lasting attachment.

Available in Fe (steel), Zn (zinc) and Pb (lead) materials, our adhesive weights offer the same precision balancing as our clip-on range, in a convenient peel-and-stick format for modern alloy wheels.

With over 12 years of manufacturing experience, each adhesive wheel weight undergoes rigorous quality testing to ensure accurate weight tolerance and reliable adhesive performance in all weather conditions.
