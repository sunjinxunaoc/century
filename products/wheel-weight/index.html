Professional Wheel Weight Manufacturer Since 2010

Hebei Century Auto Parts Co., Ltd. is a professional wheel weight manufacturer producing precision-balanced wheel weights in three materials: Fe (steel), Zn (zinc) and Pb (lead), in both clip-on and adhesive types.

Our catalog includes 2000+ different products. Wheel weights are available in gram and oz series, with multiple surface finishes (zinc plated, epoxy coated, black coated) and tape options (blue tape, white tape, 3M tape, Norton tape, easy-peel tape).
