Mushroom Patch Plug

Professional Combination Tire Patch Plugs for Permanent Tire Repairs

Premium Mushroom Tire Patch Plugs for Professional Repairs

Century Auto Parts manufactures professional mushroom patch plugs — the industry standard for permanent tire puncture repairs. Our mushroom-shaped combination patch plugs feature a stem that fills the puncture channel and a patch base that bonds to the inner tire surface.

Unlike simple tire seal strips that only seal from outside, our mushroom tire patches provide a complete internal repair that meets industry safety standards. Each plug is self-vulcanizing, creating a chemical bond with the tire rubber for a permanent, leak-proof repair.

Available in multiple stem diameters to accommodate different puncture sizes. Suitable for passenger car tires, light truck tires, and SUV tires.
