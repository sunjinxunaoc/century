Radial Tire Patch

Euro-Style Radial Patches for Professional Radial Tire Repairs

Premium Radial Tire Patches for Modern Tire Repairs

Century Auto Parts manufactures high-quality radial tire patches designed specifically for modern radial tire construction. Our Euro-style radial patches feature superior flexibility and bonding strength for permanent tire repairs.

Unlike bias patches, our radial patches are engineered to flex with the tire's radial construction, preventing patch separation during use. Made from self-vulcanizing rubber compound that creates a chemical bond with the tire inner liner.

Available in multiple sizes to accommodate different repair areas. Suitable for passenger car tires, light truck tires, and heavy-duty truck tires.
