Tire Seal Strip

Quality Rubber Tire Seal Strips for Emergency Tubeless Tire Repairs

Professional Tire Seal Strips for Quick Puncture Repairs

Century Auto Parts manufactures high-quality tire seal strips designed for quick emergency repairs of tubeless tire punctures. Our tire seal strips are made from durable rubber compound that creates a reliable seal without requiring vulcanization.

Each tire seal strip is designed for easy insertion using a standard tire repair tool. Simply insert the strip into the puncture hole, trim the excess, and the tire is ready for inflation. Perfect for roadside emergency repairs and tire shop use.

Available in multiple sizes to accommodate different puncture diameters. Bulk packaging available for tire shops and distributors, with custom branding options.
