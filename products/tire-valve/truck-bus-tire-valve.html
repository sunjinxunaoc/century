Heavy-Duty Metal Tire Valves for Commercial Vehicles

Durable Truck & Bus Tire Valves

Century Auto Parts manufactures heavy-duty truck and bus tire valves designed for the demanding conditions of commercial vehicles. Our valves feature solid brass construction for excellent pressure resistance and corrosion protection.

Available in standard TR415 and TR418 series, plus rubber base options for tubeless applications. Each valve is precision-machined and tested to meet international quality standards.

Product Specifications

Series: TR415, TR418, TR425 (available on request)

Material: Brass body, nickel plated option available

Valve Core: Standard TR412A core (V2-02-152)

Pressure Rating: Up to 10 bar (145 psi)

Application: Trucks, buses, trailers, heavy commercial vehicles

Accessories: Valve caps, cores, extensions available

Packaging: Box, bulk or custom packaging with branding
