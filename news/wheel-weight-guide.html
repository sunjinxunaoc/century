How to Choose Wheel Balancing Weights: Fe vs Pb, Clip-On vs Adhesive

Published: August 8, 2026 By Century Auto Parts

Wheel balancing weights are a small but critical part of every tyre service. Choosing the right type for your customer's wheel is the difference between a smooth, vibration-free ride and premature tyre and suspension wear. This guide explains the two decisions that matter most: material and mounting type.

Fe vs Zn vs Pb: which material?

We manufacture wheel balancing weights in three materials:

  • Fe (steel) - cost-effective and zinc plated for rust resistance. Available with zinc plated, epoxy coated or black coated finishes. The most popular choice for high-volume markets.
  • Zn (zinc) - premium corrosion resistance for regions with harsh weather and road salt.
  • Pb (lead) - the traditional choice, offering excellent vibration damping properties.

In practice, most distributors stock both Fe and Pb ranges, with zinc as a premium option. Weight is identical for a given size regardless of material, so the choice comes down to price point, local regulations and corrosion requirements.

Clip-on vs adhesive: which mounting type?

  • Clip-on wheel weights attach to the rim flange of steel wheels using a metal clip. They are fast to fit and stay secure under heavy load, making them the standard for steel rims on cars, trucks and buses.
  • Adhesive (stick-on) wheel weights use adhesive backing to mount on the inner surface of alloy and aluminum wheels, where there is no rim flange to clip onto.

The rule is simple: steel rims take clip-on weights, alloy rims take adhesive weights.

Weight range and series

Our wheel balancing weights range from 5g to 500g and are available in both gram and OZ series, covering passenger cars, light trucks, buses and commercial vehicles.

Packing and private label

We supply wheel weights in standard cartons or retail-ready packing, with private label branding available for distributors. See our Fe wheel balancing weights and lead wheel balancing weights ranges for the full model list.
