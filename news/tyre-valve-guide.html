Tyre Valve Types Explained: TR Series, Truck & Bus, Agricultural

Published: August 1, 2026 By Century Auto Parts

Tyre valves are identified by series numbers that indicate their application, bore size and stem style. Knowing which series fits which vehicle helps distributors stock the right range and avoid costly mix-ups. Here is a quick reference to the main series we manufacture.

Passenger car and motorcycle valves

  • TR412, TR413, TR414, TR414C, TR415 - the standard rubber valve series for passenger cars and light trucks.
  • PVR40, PVR50, PVR60, PVR70 - motorcycle and scooter valves.

Truck and bus valves

  • V3-20-4, V3-20-6, V3-20-22-1/58MS - high-pressure metal bus valves.
  • TR570, TR571, TR572, TR571C, TR572C - metal truck and bus valves.
  • MS70-7 - a metal valve for heavy-duty commercial applications.

Agricultural and off-road valves

  • TR618A - heavy-duty agricultural valve with a high pressure rating.
  • TRJ670, TRJ671, TRJ672 - tractor valves.
  • TR1175A - extra-large off-road valve for heavy machinery.
