Tyre Patch vs Plug vs Mushroom Patch Plug: Which to Use

Published: July 18, 2026 By Century Auto Parts

Not every puncture needs the same repair. Choosing the right method affects both the quality of the repair and how long it lasts. Here is the practical difference between the three most common repair products.

Tyre plug (seal strip)

A tyre plug is a rubber strip inserted from outside the tyre to fill a puncture. It creates an immediate seal and is ideal for quick, temporary, roadside repairs.

Tyre patch

A tyre patch is applied to the inner liner of the tyre, providing a permanent repair. Patches come in several forms:

  • Euro-type radial patches - for radial tyres.
  • Bias patches - for bias-ply tyres.
  • Tubeless tyre patches - for tubeless applications.

Mushroom patch plug

The mushroom patch plug combines both methods in one piece: a stem fills the puncture from inside while a patch bonds to the inner liner. It is widely considered the most reliable permanent repair for passenger car and light truck tyres.

See our tyre patch range and mushroom patch plugs for sizes and availability.

How to store tyre repair products

Patches and sealants rely on a self-vulcanizing rubber compound that loses effectiveness if stored incorrectly. Keep them in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and heat sources. The ideal storage temperature is 15-25°C (59-77°F).
